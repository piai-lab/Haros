# Official ACP SDK calibration

OpenCode remains the selected external Engine, but the superseded candidate's handwritten ACP
wire is not acceptance authority. The replacement must use exact `@agentclientprotocol/sdk` as the
sole framing, schema, request-correlation, handler-dispatch and cancellation authority. OmniMind
retains only bounded process/resource/security supervision, OpenCode-to-Product normalization and
its differentiated dispatch receipt, recovery, no-replay/no-fallback and visible Product truth.

Adoption requires responsibility fit without a competing authority, pinned source/version and
reproducible evidence, legal/Notice compatibility, a bounded dependency/upgrade surface and lower
lifetime complexity than custom ownership. The implementation must delete the handwritten wire and
prove the absence of a reimplementation wrapper. Work, handoff and replacement Review must record
the adopted owner, retained Product policy, rejected alternative or falsifier, license/version and
duplicate-authority residual scan. Product consolidation and any non-destructive developer-data
rebaseline remain a later Bundle and generation.
