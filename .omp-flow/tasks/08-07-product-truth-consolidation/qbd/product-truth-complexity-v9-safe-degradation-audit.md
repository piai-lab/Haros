**Consequence.** The v9 declaration hard gate must reject its own required B0 before any candidate
exists. An implementer can proceed only by changing the frozen expected phase, ignoring the hard

non-self-authorizing declaration boundary and prevents the accepted B0 report required before B1.
This directly affects PRD A9/A14 and the decision to authorize the measurement Work.

**Decision.** Blocking. The prior declaration/signature blocker is semantically degraded correctly,
but its exact B0 declaration data is still false.

**Minimum repair.** Change exactly these two rows to B0-absent and author
