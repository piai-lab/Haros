# Product-truth complexity v8 stop-loss

## Question and decision leverage

This Concept answers the bounded question returned by the immutable
[v8 r17 Review](../reviews/product-truth-complexity-v8.md): after the final callback/global grammar
counterexample, should the next Design choose exactly one of:

- **Route A:** replace the current raw/global/alias implementation with one declarative closed grammar
  and a generated exhaustive matrix; or
- **Route B:** narrow the candidate meter to candidate-independent structural, authority,
  dependency/import and count facts, and make B1 owner-local capabilities, verifier-owned real tests,
  different-actor source review and hidden mutation own combination semantics.

The decision can change the measurement boundary and the next Architect's Design. It cannot change
the human's destructive-risk ordering, Product scope, five-Work order, protected exclusions, B1
behavior, production code or any immutable v1-v8 artifact.

## Conclusion

**Select Route B.** Keep v8 r17 as immutable failed evidence and design a new, narrower meter
version; do not patch v8 again. Preserve exact Work membership, accepted-predecessor/evidence
binding, outside-Work blob equality, path lifecycle, declaration/interface authority, dependency and
static import/export closure, public non-leak shape, import/SCC/LOC and stable Product counts. Remove
raw/global/alias **expression-combination interpretation** and its derived per-use owner verdict from
the candidate meter. Make the already-required B1 capability/verifier/source-review system own
whether every real effect is mediated, correctly ordered, non-leaking and safe.

This **confirms** the r17 stop-loss and the v7 first-principles split between mechanical facts and
runtime behavior, but **revises** that split one step further: lexical raw-use composition is not a
bounded candidate gate for this checkpoint, even though each individual AST counterexample is
finite. It does not prove Route A impossible; it finds Route A unjustified under the current Occam
and stop-loss constraints.

The evidence does not change the Product problem, destructive allowlist, protected facts or Work
ordering, so no return to Brainstorm is warranted. It does require return to Design because the
current Design names v8 as the sole candidate authority and assigns expression-combination facts to
it (`design.md:626-668`).

## Confirmed facts

### 1. Product and proof ownership already favor a narrow governance tool

The current execution authority says Product truth consolidation must reuse the existing
SQLite/LevelDB, atomic-file, locking, Package-generation and owner mechanisms; the unique gap is
authority consolidation, direct first-public behavior, stable responsibility separation and proved
net reduction. It explicitly forbids an unbounded semantic meter and assigns proof to exact
destructive allowlists, real fault/race tests and different-actor source review
(`execution-brief.md:95-103`). The same owner says governance scripts, meters and fixtures are tools,
not a second product, and requires one of the two present routes after repeated adjacent findings
(`execution-brief.md:27`). The current checkpoint and downstream ordering remain unchanged
(`execution-brief.md:132-138`; `README.md:315-325`).

This is consistent with the architecture's broader authority rule: real side effects remain owned by
their actual filesystem/Engine/remote authorities, and product proof must not create a competing
truth (`architecture/product-state.md:3-23`).

### 2. V4-v6 falsified meter-owned runtime interpretation

V4 passed all 90 authored tests and deterministic mechanical checks, but five hidden successes showed
that its claimed semantic proof substituted source order and token names for CFG/capability facts
(`reviews/product-truth-complexity-v4.md:21-35`). The hidden cases included two distinct branch
acquisitions collapsed by one spelling, a detached callback release, an unreachable refusal and a
swallowed refusal (`reviews/product-truth-complexity-v4.md:63-102`).

V5 passed all 107 authored tests but eight single-change hidden counterexamples bypassed classifier
origin/cleanup, scheduled release, loop acquisition and reset completion
(`reviews/product-truth-complexity-v5.md:21-39`). V6 then claimed a shared bounded ICFG yet still
accepted unsafe classifier/scratch/scheduler/reset overlays and rejected safe controls; inspection
found category-specific scans, source-position checks and independent callback lists behind the
unified name (`reviews/product-truth-complexity-v6.md:156-167`). The v6 Review's requested repair was
effectively a completion-aware value/resource/task/exception interpreter
(`reviews/product-truth-complexity-v6.md:189-196`).

These versions establish more than “the tests were incomplete”: their proof owner was wrong. Adding
runtime semantic combinations to a governance meter repeatedly reconstructed a partial program
analyzer while still failing bounded hidden mutations.

### 3. V7 succeeded only after the semantic claim was removed

The v7 authority deliberately restricted itself to frozen membership, byte-bound dependency/import
closure, syntactic ingress/owner shape, complexity/count facts and deterministic reports
(`interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v7.md:19-39`). It explicitly delegated same-module misuse,
runtime-derived escape and lifecycle safety to the B1 verifier and source Review
(`interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v7.md:170-195`). R5 received a zero-finding `PASS` and explicitly
made no CFG, order, lifetime or runtime claim (`reviews/product-truth-complexity-v7.md:20-45`).

V7's accepted B0 snapshot is still useful: v8 records 812 ingress sites and 712 observational owner
violations across 93 paths without declaring them B1-safe (`interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v8.md:10-28`).
Its later-candidate verdict failed for a different reason—unchanged outside-Work violations and
ignored declared symbols—not because the B0 inventory was nondeterministic.

### 4. V8's finite grammar nevertheless became a combination interpreter

V8's declared limit is structural: syntax, lexical declarations, raw-terminal normalization, static
imports, blobs and multisets only (`interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v8.md:38-47`). In practice,
the candidate verdict must combine declaration kind, lexical ancestry, global-wrapper/selector
normalization, assignment/destructure/property handling, callback ownership, per-use alias identity,
AST roles, expression fingerprints, sibling anchors and relative order
(`interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v8.md:185-221`). Those independently finite dimensions compose
into a large cross-product.

cross-product boundary, not 17 independent product requirements.

R17 is especially diagnostic. It closes direct raw arguments, yet its callback boundary suppresses
the entire function subtree while the alternate visitor recognizes globals only in call/new callee

make the current combination surface bounded enough.

### 5. B1 already has a stronger, correctly placed behavior proof owner

The B1 Work does not merely promise later tests. It already requires each exact owner to expose a
non-leaking typed capability, keep paths/handles/batches/locks/releases/process adapters private and
accept verifier-owned deterministic ports/events/faults/race/kill controls
(`work/direct-first-public-b1.md:59-80`). The Design-owned universe freezes 10 owners, 146 operations,
87 states, 34 barriers expanded to 85 race cases, and 29 durable kill identities expanded to 65 kill
cases (`work/direct-first-public-b1.md:74-80`).

The verifier must derive and execute the manifest rather than accept candidate-filtered samples,
assert trace prefixes, terminal disposition, writes, post-state, exclusions and sanitized outputs,
and use generated homes only (`interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v7.md:951-975`). A fresh different
actor must also mutate same-owner raw-port bypass, outside-owner import/loader, skipped cleanup,
wrong resource, early release, swallowed failure, reduced catalog cardinality and missing race/kill
convergence, then source-review every capability, port composition, raw ingress reference and matrix
at the same SHA (`interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v7.md:977-990`). The B1 Work repeats these exact
requirements and says meter success cannot substitute for manifest bijection or source review
(`work/direct-first-public-b1.md:286-301`; `work/direct-first-public-b1.md:337-342`).

The failed historical B1 candidate further shows why real production inspection matters: green
focused tests and faithful static measurements did not catch a wrong composed database path,
missing runtime refusal, replacement-race deletion or Unix-only quiescence
(`reviews/direct-first-public-b1.md:20-33`). Concrete entry-point inspection and a real replacement
race did (`reviews/direct-first-public-b1.md:37-56`; `reviews/direct-first-public-b1.md:90-123`).

## Interpretation and strongest counter-case

### What the evidence means

The principal contradiction is not “static analysis versus no static analysis.” It is **claim
ownership**: a governance meter is trying to decide expression combinations that are already more
strongly observable at the owner capability, injected-port trace and same-SHA source-review boundary.
This delays the sole Product checkpoint while adding a parallel assurance implementation.

Route B does not treat `open` as absent and does not propose rebuilding the Product capability. It
reuses the accepted v7 mechanical base and the already-designed B1 verifier, then wires the remaining
OmniMind gap: one exact, independently selected candidate/predecessor boundary plus real proof that
effects stay behind the declared owner capabilities. This follows the repository's required
reuse → gap → wire/local repair → replacement order (`execution-brief.md:17-25`).

### Strongest case against Route B

R17's counterexample can introduce `Bun.file` or an unresolved global chain inside an authorized
owner without changing a static import edge. If the narrower meter checks only files, declarations,
dependencies and coarse counts, an implementation could bypass the injected port and still compile.
Hidden mutation or human source review after the fact is weaker than a deterministic hard gate when
its detection condition is not mandatory and reproducible.

This counter-case is real. Route B is acceptable only if Design binds all three compensating facts:

1. raw-capable production modules remain an exact closed owner/module set, with exact public
   capability/type non-leak contracts and no new owner/module admitted by candidate configuration;
2. verifier composition makes every declared effect observable through the injected private port,
   and required hidden mutations include direct global, wrapper, alias, callback and same-owner raw
   bypasses from all r1-r17 families; and
3. the different-actor Review has a mandatory same-SHA source scan of **every raw root/import/loader
   reference and adapter composition**, with an explicit zero-unexplained-reference result—not a
   prose assurance or sampled review.

If the Architect cannot make these conditions deterministic without rebuilding the same expression
grammar inside another script, Route B is falsified and Route A must be reconsidered.

## Route-specific falsifiers

### Route A would become justified only if

- the Design can express one closed grammar algebra with no parallel special-case visitors;
- code is generated from that algebra, every supported TypeScript AST production and lexical context
  maps to exactly one disposition, recursion/unsupported forms terminate fail-closed, and the tests
  are generated from the same authority rather than appended by Review family;
- an independent mutation pass across the r1-r17 families finds no miss and no harmless-control false
  rejection; and
- the total analyzer/matrix lifecycle is demonstrably smaller than the B1 proof it would replace.

Any new post-freeze finding answered by another authored syntax exception, a second visitor, or a
fixture family not derivable from the grammar falsifies Route A's claimed closure.

### Route B is falsified if

- a selected-Work candidate can add a second or bypass raw effect while preserving the retained
  Work/blob/declaration/dependency/import/public-surface/count facts, and neither the generated B1
  verifier nor the mandatory raw-reference/source review has a deterministic failure condition;
- any destructive/runtime effect cannot be mediated by an exact owner-private port or observed by a
  real-process/fault/race/kill proof without exposing a raw capability;
- the “narrow” meter reintroduces alias, selector, callback or expression-RHS combination verdicts
  under another name; or
- source review becomes sampled prose, candidate-filtered, different-SHA, or unable to enumerate all
  raw roots/imports/loaders and adapter compositions.

These falsifiers are design-entry conditions, not reasons to run B1 before its gate exists.

## Architect handoff

The next Architect can proceed without reconstructing this research by applying the following
bounded decision:

1. Preserve v1-v7 and failed v8 r1-r17 bytes, handoffs and Reviews unchanged. Introduce a new meter
   version; do not relabel r17 or repair v8 in place.
2. Revise PRD R11/A14 and Design's measurement section so the new meter owns only:
   - immutable Design/Work boundary extraction and exact membership;
   - Main/human-selected predecessor evidence, reviewed-candidate/evidence/candidate ancestry and
     exact evidence blobs/report tuple;
   - selected-Work lifecycle and exact outside-Work presence/mode/blob equality;
   - exact declaration/interface identities and public non-leak shape for owner capabilities;
   - manifest/lock/source-digest dependency closure and resolved static import/export edges;
   - forbidden outside-set/computed import/loader edges that are decidable without expression-use
     propagation;
   - LOC/import/SCC and stable Product counts, including writers, connections, tables, facade
     operations, durable states and forbidden compatibility identities.
3. Remove candidate-verdict authority for global wrapper normalization, per-use raw alias
   propagation, expression subtree/RHS classification, callback owner inheritance and raw structural
   site fingerprints. Historical B0 ingress remains provenance, not a B1/C behavioral verdict.
4. Keep exact raw-capable owner modules and capability declarations closed in Design. Bind B1 to
   owner-private real/verifier ports, typed intent, sanitized result and no raw public surface.
5. Expand the already-required hidden-mutation list—not the meter grammar—to include every v8 r1-r17
   bypass family, especially the four r17 callback-global forms. Require the real verifier, retained
   import/dependency/public-shape gate, or explicit same-SHA raw-reference source review to reject
   each mutation while adjacent positives pass.
6. Make the different-actor Review emit a deterministic inventory/count/digest of every raw
   root/import/loader reference and production adapter composition reviewed at the B1 SHA, while
   keeping reviewer expectations and mutation verdicts outside candidate production/meter bytes.
7. Update the B1 entry stop to consume the new different-actor-accepted meter and accepted B0
   predecessor. Do not weaken destructive exclusions, real replacement-race/process tests, the
   87-state/146-operation/85-race/65-kill manifest, or the Product-truth-first execution order.

This is a Design change only. No B1 implementation, destructive use, Product edit or acceptance
promotion is authorized by this research.

## Unknowns that remain bounded

- Whether an exact public API/type non-leak gate can be expressed with the existing TypeScript
  compiler surface without rebuilding per-expression alias flow. The Architect must prove the
  boundary or reduce it to exact exported declarations plus source review.
- Whether all current owner production adapters can be constructed through the verifier-owned port
  interface without a legitimate unobservable raw reference. Current Design asserts this, but B1 has
  not yet implemented it.
- The generated B1 manifest's actual runtime cost. Its authority is already fixed; focused execution
  and sharding may optimize runtime but may not filter cases or reduce bijection.
- The smallest deterministic format for same-SHA raw-reference review evidence. It must expose
  coverage/digest and sanitized dispositions without turning reviewer expectations into candidate
  authority.

None changes the route selection. Each has an explicit Route-B falsifier and belongs in Design/QbD
before the next meter or B1 assignment.
