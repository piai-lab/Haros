# Freeze the authoritative Product-truth v6 meter

## Objective

Replace rejected immutable v5 measurement authority with one narrow v6 meter. V6 preserves the
accepted frozen universe, authority roots, semantic counters and complete inherited matrix, while
replacing classifier-specific proof, source-position fallback and static-callsite lock identity
with one shared resolved-symbol, event/resource-based bounded ICFG/SSA/points-to must-analysis. This
Work implements measurement only.
