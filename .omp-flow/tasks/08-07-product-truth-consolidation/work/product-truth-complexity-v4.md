# Freeze the authoritative Product-truth v4 meter

## Objective

Replace rejected immutable v3 measurement authority with a new immutable v4 meter before any
repaired B1 production work. V4 preserves the accepted frozen-universe and candidate-graph model,
but discovers the complete resolved database opener/wrapper/caller/handle graph before Product
classification and proves the Product/service two-stage and Web single-stage refusal cuts over
every reachable current-generation I/O sink. The capability graph is independently rooted in the
accepted source/dependency closure, and Product/service proof additionally requires the matching
canonical owner lock to remain definitely held through every guarded sink. This Work implements
measurement only.

## Done conditions

- Config extraction, Design pinning, normalized five-Work blocks/digests, one-time glob expansion,
  frozen membership, dependency integrity and candidate-time graph rules satisfy the v4 interface.
  It also extracts, normalizes and pins the capability and owner-lock authority blocks, then
  independently
  re-derives the complete reachable primitive/opener/dynamic-loader/layer/factory/wrapper/receiver
  inventory from accepted Work membership, exact `bun.lock` resolution and dependency source/JS/
  d.ts bytes. Per-seed/path-slot/handle-edge omission or mutation, dependency drift and new
  unresolved persistence flow fail before Product classification.
- The meter enumerates every invocation in that derived database capability inventory before
  Product classification; closed interprocedural path and handle summaries cover wrappers, callers,
  aliases, returns, receivers and branch merges. Generic wrapper summaries are instantiated per
  resolved caller context; only exact approved canonical-service, scratch-copy or memory origins can
  prove non-Product authority. No text/Product-name prefilter or author-selected sink list participates.
- Every Product component is inside frozen membership and has canonical-resolver-only provenance.
  Generic wrapper plus raw Product path, constructor alias and mixed caller/handle provenance fail.
- For exact Product and service owner entries, complete main/WAL/SHM pre-mutation cuts dominate
  current stores mkdir/file/lock and complete post-lock cuts dominate every subsequent
  read/create/open/write/receiver sink while the exact matching canonical owner/lane/root/database
  lock capability is definitely held. Acquire/release identities, aliases, scoped Effect/Layer
  finalizers and normal/throw/catch/finally exits are resolved; no release or unknown lock flow
  precedes a guarded sink. The Web v1/v2 cut dominates all g1 sinks. Present legacy cannot reach
  current I/O on any reachable path.
- The complete inherited and new v4 adversarial matrix passes/fails for its intended single cause.
  The two rejected-v3 counterexamples, a missing sidecar, preguard without postguard, branch/catch/
  finally/deferred bypass and a raw Product path behind neutral names all fail. Real Effect SQLite
  loader/layer coverage, per-derived-identity authority mutations and a new `SqliteMigrator` are
  included. Dropped acquire, early release, wrong owner/lane/root/lock-path/token, sibling/direct-
  tool handle, aliased release and finally-before-sink each fail independently;
  positive direct and Effect scoped-finalizer shapes pass. Unsupported callback/Effect/recursion/
  control or lock-flow edges fail closed.
- One clean dedicated commit contains only allowed v4 paths. The handoff records its full SHA,
  accepted Design SHA, script/config/boundary/universe digests and byte-deterministic B0 report.
  Exact B0 reports historical semantic red without weakening authority/integrity failures; every
  other candidate and focused semantic fixture enforces the same findings as hard failures.
