# Product-truth complexity v9 Route B safe-degradation calibration

## Human decision

The maintainer selects the safe-degradation branch of Route B in response to the independent
[v9 audit](../qbd/product-truth-complexity-v9-audit.md). The two audit blockers and its advisory are
accepted as Design defects to repair, not as accepted implementation risk. This decision preserves
the earlier [Route B stop-loss](product-truth-complexity-v9-stop-loss-calibration.md): v9 remains a
narrow repository measurement and must not regain raw/global/alias/callback/RHS/per-use grammar.

## Calibrated v9 authority

- Declaration hard gates are limited to exact path, symbol, declaration kind, exact
  exported/module-private disposition, explicit B0 presence or absence, and a Design-authored first
  materialization Work for every future declaration. A candidate, meter or config cannot create its
  own expected declaration. An emitted signature is hard only when independently existing bytes and
  their digest were pinned by Design before the candidate; candidate-emitted or inferred type
  closure is observational.
- Static graph output is the complete sorted multiset of literal `import` and `export ... from`
  records under the Design-frozen production-member universe. It is hard only when Design pins the
  baseline digest and every exact allowed delta with its phase disposition. The present authority
  has no such complete delta table, so graph differences, SCCs and domain interpretations are
  observational. Labels such as raw non-leak, write edge, Web/RPC-only facade or gateway ownership
  are not v9 predicates.
- Membership, official predecessor evidence, selected-Work lifecycle, outside-Work
  presence/mode/blob equality, dependency/source-byte closure, declaration identity/disposition,
  deterministic report production and physical LOC/count observations retain only the exact
  authority stated by the revised [v9 Interface](../interfaces/product-truth-complexity-v9.md).

## B1 acceptance owns displaced safety

B1 acceptance—not v9—hard-fails public raw-capability leakage, Native Host Package-lifecycle writes,
unmediated raw effects, incomplete enumeration, any unexplained raw reference and every required
negative mutation that is not rejected while its adjacent positive passes. At the identical B1
SHA, the different actor must retain the exact reviewer-owned enumeration command, runtime/tool
version, enumerator source bytes and digest, Design-frozen source-universe digest, canonical raw-
reference records, sorted count/JCS digest, source Review dispositions and the full immutable v8
r1-r17 mutation manifest with adjacent positives. Candidate code/config may neither supply nor
filter that universe, command, expectations or results.

Any new raw bypass that preserves every v9 fact and escapes the fixed enumeration, generated
verifier and same-SHA source Review falsifies Route B. It returns to Design and must never be
answered by another v9 grammar patch.

## Preserved boundary and next gate

V1-v8 artifacts, production and existing meter bytes, Reviews, handoffs, all five production fence
blocks, the destructive allowlist, protected exclusions, Product Work order and the three current
Synara-first owner documents remain byte-identical. This decision authorizes no meter
implementation, B1, destructive execution or Product edit.

The next entry is a fresh different-actor QbD over the repaired PRD, Design, both Route B Decisions,
v9 Interface/Work, five Product Works and authored Work map. It must report **0 blocker and 0
advisory** before a human PASS calibration may authorize v9 implementation or B1.
