# Repair the complete design after QbD 1

The independent [QbD 1 audit](../qbd/design-audit.md) returned `FAIL`. The human reviewed the
material findings and, on 2026-08-03, explicitly selected the recommended repair path.

## Decision

Repair the full scope and request a fresh, scoped QbD 1 re-audit after the substantive design
change. Do not narrow or defer the Package/UI surface, weaken the one-owner doctrine, delete or
mutate the exact fixed-source baseline, or advance the unchanged design to decomposition.

The repair must close all three blocking findings:

1. make the future Workbench owner complete for onboarding, provenance, Models, Agents, Packages,
   permission policy versus enforcement truth, external-Engine capability/no-fallback behavior,
   and the fixed plugin/skill discovery lineage;
2. reduce `AGENTS.md` to routing and fail-closed working rules, and reduce `execution-brief.md` to
   order and stage gates, with durable topology and product objects owned only by architecture;
3. design an executable quality boundary in which declared exact source-adoption roots are
   provenance evidence rather than production author structure, while exactness, rights and donor
   leakage outside those roots remain checked.

The repair must also resolve the audit's evidence and advisory observations: use an immutable Git
base for changed-path proof, remove stale instructions to repeat unchanged desktop smoke, and make
the mandatory read route consistent.
