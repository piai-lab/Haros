was observed read-only only for shell geometry, visible entry vocabulary, density, and the existence
of the left work rail, central conversation, bottom composer, and Steer/Add action controls. No real

## Replacement-family rule

Every slot uses a Haros-grounded, information-dense professional technical editorial diagram or
interface-anatomy plate on a large clean white field. It must combine architecture cross-sections,
process decomposition, state transitions, component relationships, inputs/outputs,
failure/recovery paths, invariants/limits, and restrained factual callouts. The palette is
low-saturation neutral gray and teal with sparse warm amber accents. It must not impersonate a
Haros screen.

Rejected patterns are: toy tracks, storage bins, conveyor/workbench mechanisms, gears, lifebuoys,
joysticks, oversized physical metaphors, sparse three-object or three-word posters, sci-fi
dashboards, fabricated UI, polished object scenes that do not teach Haros, real or illustrated
people, human-centered scenes, desks, rooms, and workplace storytelling. Natural English capitalization and exact labels are necessary but not sufficient. All nine prior rasters are rejected and their SHA-256 values are deny-listed by
the validator.
